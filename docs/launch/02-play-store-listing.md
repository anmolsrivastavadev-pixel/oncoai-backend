# OncoAI — Play Store listing copy

> **OncoAI is an educational skin health awareness platform and does not provide medical diagnosis or treatment advice.**

This copy follows the wording rules in [04-health-policy-review.md](04-health-policy-review.md).
It assumes the product changes listed there have been made, in particular removing graded
"concern" results. If the scan screen still shows a concern level or score when you submit, the
copy below no longer describes the app accurately, and the app is likely to be treated as a
medical device.

Character limits are Google Play's (title 30, short description 80, full description 4,000).

---

## App name (≤ 30)

```
OncoAI: Skin Health Journal
```
(27 characters.) Alternative: `OncoAI – Skin Awareness` (23).

> The prefix "Onco-" means cancer. A reviewer may read the name itself as a cancer-detection
> claim. Keep the descriptive part of the title clearly about education and journaling, and see
> section 3.5 of the policy review.

## Short description (≤ 80)

```
Learn about skin health, track spots with photos and prepare for skin checks.
```
(77 characters.)

Alternatives:
- `A skin awareness journal: photo-track spots, learn the ABCDEs, set reminders.` (77)
- `Educational skin health app: photo journal, self-check guides and reminders.` (76)

## Full description (≤ 4,000)

```
OncoAI is an educational skin health awareness app. It helps you learn how to check your skin, keep a private photo journal of spots you want to watch, and get ready for conversations with a dermatologist.

OncoAI is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition. It does not tell you whether a spot is harmful or harmless. Always consult a healthcare professional for medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. If a spot is bleeding, changing quickly or worrying you, contact a doctor.

WHAT YOU CAN DO

• Keep a private skin photo journal
Photograph a spot with your camera, or add one from your gallery, and file it under a body location. Every photo is stored privately in your account. Location data and other hidden metadata are removed from photos before they are stored.

• See how a spot looks over time
Compare two photos of the same spot side by side. OncoAI describes visual features such as symmetry, border, colour and relative size, so you can notice and describe changes. These descriptions are for your own tracking and learning. They are not a medical assessment.

• Photo quality tips
Get instant feedback on blur, lighting and contrast so your photos stay consistent from one check to the next.

• Log what you notice
Record itching, bleeding, pain or changes in size, colour or shape, with your own notes, so you can share an accurate history with a clinician.

• Learn the basics
Read short guides on the ABCDE rule, how to do a skin self-exam, UV protection, skin types and what happens at a dermatologist visit.

• Reminders
Set reminders for regular self-checks, re-photographing a spot or upcoming appointments.

• Find a dermatologist
Browse dermatologist and clinic listings and keep track of your appointments.

• A supportive community
Share experiences and awareness tips with others, with the option to post anonymously. Posts are moderated, and phone numbers and email addresses are blocked to protect your privacy.

YOUR PRIVACY

• Your photos and health notes are private to your account and are never shown to other users.
• Photos are encrypted at rest and sent only over secure connections.
• No ads, and we never sell your data.
• Delete individual photos, or your whole account and all of its data, at any time from Settings.

Read our privacy policy: [https://oncoai.app/privacy]

IMPORTANT

OncoAI is an educational skin health awareness platform and does not provide medical diagnosis or treatment advice. It is not a substitute for professional medical care. Only a qualified healthcare professional can examine and diagnose skin conditions. In an emergency, contact your local emergency services.

OncoAI is intended for adults aged [18]+.
```

> Check before submitting: if the dermatologist directory still uses the fictional sample listings

## Screenshot and graphic guidelines

- Every screenshot that shows photo analysis must show the in-app disclaimer banner.
- Do not show a concern level, score, colour-coded risk badge or "result" heading in any image.
- Do not use real patients' skin photos unless you have their written consent. Use licensed or
  consented images.
- Don't use stethoscopes, white coats or hospital imagery suggesting the app is a clinical service.
- Feature graphic text suggestion: "Your skin health journal" / "Learn. Track. Talk to your dermatologist."

## Words and claims to avoid anywhere in the listing

`diagnose`, `diagnosis`, `detect`, `detection`, `screen`/`screening`, `melanoma`, `skin cancer check`,
`cancer risk`, `risk score`, `accuracy`, `% accurate`, `dermatologist-grade`, `clinically proven`,
`AI doctor`, `instant results`, `peace of mind`, `early warning`, `know if a mole is dangerous`,
`safe`/`harmless`/`benign`, `replace your doctor`. Also avoid comparisons with dermatologists'
performance.
